Wren · Pico programmer wiring

A Raspberry Pi Pico (RP2040) or Pico 2 (RP2350) as a SWIM programmer for the OLED Thing. Seen from above, USB at the top.

Connections

WirePico pinBreadboardTo
4 GP2b4Thing J2 pin 2 SWIM
5 GP3b5Thing J2 pin 3 NRST
3 GNDb3 → − railThing J2 pin 1 GND
36 3V3(OUT)j5 → + railsThing VCC (top-right corner)
1 kΩa4 → + railSWIM pull-up to 3.3 V

Parts

  • Pico or Pico 2 (W versions work too), on a half-size breadboard straddling the gap, pins in columns c and h, rows 1–20.
  • One 1 kΩ resistor: brown, black, red, gold. The Pico's own pull-up is too weak for SWIM.
  • Four jumper wires to the Thing, plus three short ones on the breadboard.

3.3 V only

  • The Thing runs from the Pico's 3V3(OUT). Nothing else may power it while the Pico is connected: the Pico's pins are not 5 V tolerant.
  • Wire everything with USB unplugged, then check each wire against the table.

Then, in Wren

  1. Plug the Pico in (the PicoLink sketch already on it: docs/PicoLink.md).
  2. Tools → Programmer…, click Find, choose Pico on COMn, click Connect. The Pico's LED blinks while it works.
  3. Back up… saves the Thing's flash, EEPROM and option bytes. Connect and Back up only read.
