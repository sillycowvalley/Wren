Wren · ST-LINK programmer wiring

An ST-LINK V2 (or a compatible dongle) as the Thing's programmer: four wires to the Thing.

Power

  • The Thing runs at 3.3 V or 5 V (2.95–5.5 V). The dongle's 3.3V or 5.0V pin powers it; use one, not both.
  • ST's own ST-LINK/V2 does not power the Thing: its SWIM connector's VDD pin only senses the voltage. Power the Thing separately and join the grounds.
  • No pull-up resistor is needed: the ST-LINK drives SWIM properly.

ST's own ST-LINK/V2

Its separate 4-pin SWIM connector: 1 VDD (sense), 2 SWIM, 3 GND, 4 RESET. Wire SWIM, GND and RESET as above, and VDD to the Thing's VCC.

In Wren

  1. Plug the ST-LINK in. Wren uses it through Windows' own driver (WinUSB).
  2. Tools → Firmware… sets the Thing up: Install kernel once.
  3. Then Debug → Target → Thing on the programmer: F5, Ctrl+F5 and the stepping keys work on the Thing.
